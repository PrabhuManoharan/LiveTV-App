# Privacy Policy — LiveTV **Last updated:** October 22, 2026 ## 1. Introduction LiveTV (“the App”) is an application that allows users to access and play compatible live television streams using supported playlist URLs or files. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled. ## 2. Information Collection and Use LiveTV does not require users to create an account unless explicitly stated within the App. The App and its third-party SDKs may process certain information, such as device information, app interactions, advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from IP address, where permitted and applicable. The information processed depends on the features used and the configuration of third-party services. ## 3. Advertising LiveTV uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. For more information, please review Google's advertising and privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 4. IPTV Playlists and Streaming Users may provide IPTV playlist URLs or files to access compatible streams. Playlist information and streaming requests may be processed locally or transmitted to relevant streaming providers, depending on the App's implementation. The privacy practices of third-party streaming services are governed by their respective policies. Users should only access streams they are authorized to view. ## 5. Data Sharing Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, for advertising, analytics, security, and related purposes where applicable. We do not claim that all information remains on the device; handling depends on the App's implementation and the third-party services used. ## 6. Data Retention and Security Data retention depends on the information involved and the services processing it. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. ## 7. Children's Privacy LiveTV is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Where applicable, advertising and data processing will comply with relevant legal requirements and Google Play policies. ## 8. Your Choices You can manage advertising preferences and available privacy controls through your device settings and applicable Google services. You can also stop using the App or remove locally stored playlists and app data through the available device settings. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date. ## 10.
